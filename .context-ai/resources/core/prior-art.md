# Prior-art decisions

Compose [engineering](engineering.md) and [research](research.md). Source-supported interfaces establish what a component does; project policy decides whether to adopt it. A local trial establishes behavior in its stated environment, not universal superiority.

Name the need, acceptance behavior and cost of a wrong choice. Inspect the current owner, language/platform primitives, relevant specifications and maintained implementations before building the residual. Compare serious options on compatibility, authority, licensing, maintenance, privacy, cost, context burden and removal. A popular component may be unsuitable; an obscure compatible component may be trial-ready.

Choose adopt, adapt, trial, build, defer or reject with a short rationale, supported claims, applicability, unknowns, verification, rollback and a specific reassessment trigger. Use an existing decision format. Stop when credible evidence and one discriminating test answer the actual boundary; do not require an exhaustive survey or multiple independent studies for a reversible low-risk trial.

Defer when a consequential unknown cannot be tested within authority. Do not leave an endless list of candidates as a decision. Append a successor when observations change the choice, preserving prior evidence and pins. See the [house policy](../overlays/prior-art.md) for its selected mechanism order.
