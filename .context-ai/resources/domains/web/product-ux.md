# Product, UX and content

Project policy informed by Impeccable: derive audience, primary job, primary action, navigation and trust from existing product truth. Reuse authoritative product/design documents. Fill ordinary design gaps with recorded choices; ask only when a missing fact blocks truthful content or an external action.

Use semantic reading order, clear actions and consistent terminology. Treat loading, empty, validation, success, error and recovery states as part of the feature. Preserve entered data on recoverable failure. Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, certifications or outcomes. Do not claim contact submission works after a mocked happy path; verify the configured backend or honestly offer an email link.

Test long labels, keyboard interaction, touch and small screens. Distinct layouts should follow information hierarchy, not decoration. Browser feedback can disprove a plausible wireframe.
