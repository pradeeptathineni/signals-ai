# Signals product

Discover, explain, retain and keep current worthwhile knowledge for personal interests and projects.

[product.yaml](product.yaml) is the canonical product ledger. All 23 rows are required; a supporting role does not make a feature optional. Delivery describes working behavior. Value describes only the recorded scope: 1 is implementation, 2 is functional real use, 3 is a concrete scoped benefit and 4 requires evidence of preference over a credible alternative. Unknown value is null. Guardrails pass or fail separately.

The local workbench connects Search Signals, Inbox, Corpus, Interests, Sources, Jobs and Settings. Ordinary research uses one native task and retains its original answer before candidate validation. Scores, evidence and limitations remain inspectable. Explicitly saved provisional findings remain available alongside established resources.

Private evaluation artifacts stay in the owner planning folder. Paths prefixed private: identify those retained receipts and are not published contents. The ledger does not establish personal novelty or human usefulness without feedback.
