# Signals product

Discover, explain, retain and keep current worthwhile knowledge for personal interests and projects.

[product.yaml](product.yaml) is the canonical product ledger. All 25 rows are required; a supporting role does not make a feature optional. Delivery describes working behavior. Value describes only the recorded scope: 1 is implementation, 2 is functional real use, 3 is a concrete scoped benefit and 4 requires evidence of preference over a credible alternative. Unknown value is null. Guardrails pass or fail separately.

The local workbench opens on Home with retained interest discoveries, Library and recent searches. Library, Online and Combined scopes distinguish retained knowledge from fresh research. Submitted Library search keeps immediate literal results and can use one bounded no-web meaning match after metadata-sharing permission. Projects retains scoped contexts and opportunities; Following, Activity and Settings retain operational controls. Online research uses one native task and retains its original answer before candidate validation. Capture, Inbox, comparison, frozen-evidence follow-up and historical jobs remain reachable.

[ADR-017](architecture/ADR-017-query-assessment-and-library.md) owns the current query relevance and type-specific Signal policies. Manual private saving includes unassessed leads. Automatic personal saving requires an enabled preference, completed assessment and supported relevance; it is off by default. Historical ledger assessments below retain their original evidence and source pins. Initial weights and mechanical validation do not establish calibrated quality, research superiority or personal usefulness.

General project opportunities moved from Context with their original records and provenance. Context retains AI equipment selection, permissions, pins, delivery, verification and undo. A Signals AI setup request is advisory and does not execute a candidate or install anything. Earlier feature assessments retain their original pins; experience receipts add scoped observations rather than replacing historical value evidence.

Private evaluation artifacts stay in the owner planning folder. Paths prefixed private: identify those retained receipts and are not published contents. The ledger does not establish personal novelty or human usefulness without feedback.
