# ADR-014: native briefs and bounded retained research

Status: accepted for local use, 2026-10-08. Historical policies and schema-v1 interchange remain unchanged.

Ordinary research in an existing AI session uses the invoking signal-search skill. It does not start another researcher. Headless search and new local profiles use one Sol low native brief session, followed by deterministic validation and selective source checks. Existing explicit settings remain authoritative; new profiles save privately and leave recurrence disabled. Full assessment remains explicit, and Deep requests additional grounded interpretation. CLI `--accept` and `--schema` retain their legacy full-proposal defaults; compact clients select `--route native`. Native brief v2 encodes exact existing feature/indicator pairs; v1 remains readable. Unknown dimensions earn no invented credit.

Two frozen cases at f20a593 maintained important useful coverage: the brief added tokf/Cordon options; direct native research supplied better nearby referral detail. The brief took 191/148 seconds versus native 123/130 seconds. It earns a narrower product role through source-bound inspectable records, explicit retention and reuse, not research speed or universal superiority. Invoking native research remains the recommended route when already in an AI host. Sol low discovery is retained. Luna's 8-second frozen synthesis was useful but its 65-second live Queens run lost dated conversation choices and contradicted its fee caveat; Luna is not promoted. The same-model evaluator did not write the product but prepared the cases; human value and served-model identity remain unknown.

Research receives at most five relevant source-bound saved records before acquisition. Explicit primary checks compare conditional HTTP validators or normalized hashes without model interpretation, renewing no claims and making no claim about independent changes. Transport validators survive cache and store round trips. An observed changed representation requires interpretation. Exact duplicate query-list needs share one acquisition within an activation, with each interest retaining scope/provenance and uncertainty. Different contexts remain separate; broader semantic grouping is left to native query planning rather than unsafe deterministic inference.

Fetched content, scores, query utility and admission remain separate. Rejected proposals retain their raw structured output and bounded unvalidated lead text; they do not become entities. Provisional leads are unsavable. Source URLs may include public resource query parameters, while supplemental search scopes remain query-free. Credentials, private hosts, ambient hooks/plugins/cookies, candidate execution and automatic installation remain outside research authority.

No new runtime dependency, canonical database, embedding service, scheduler or agent platform was added. Existing safe transport, extraction, lexical retrieval, stateful writer, timers and public Pages remain. Source inspections and exact phase evidence are recorded in the sister planning folder's canonical RESET state/results, not loaded as permanent instructions. Optional providers and human usefulness remain separately qualified.
