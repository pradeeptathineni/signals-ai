# Legacy Signals research compatibility

Use [signal-search](../../signal-search/SKILL.md) for live discovery and native entity scores. That protocol owns current research, saving and the local workbench.

`npm run research -- corpus-query "QUESTION"` preserves literal legacy Corpus queries. Its optional explicit loopback model organizes and challenges known evidence; it does not acquire live sources.

Legacy `curate` and `admit` preserve `signal-review-v1` and schema-v1 interchange for retained findings. Their all-strong gate must not be applied to native entities or presented as the current strength policy.

Historical evidence remains immutable. Private queries, drafts and retained snapshots stay under ignored `.signals/`. See [the product contract](../../../../docs/signals-contract.md) for current and historical boundaries.
